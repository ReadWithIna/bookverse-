About Me

Hi guys, this is my first web app — yay! It may not be perfect, but it's my baby.

As someone with no background in coding and its shenanigans, I'd like to think I did pretty good; and what better tiny app to build on than something I love: reading.

I'm Mildred, but I also go by Ina. I created this Book Hub to share my love for reading and to connect with others who enjoy discovering new stories. This site is my personal hub where I write reviews, share recommendations, and talk about the books that inspire me.

Why I Read

Books are my way of traveling through time, meeting unforgettable characters, and finding pieces of myself in every story. This hub is my invitation for you to join me on that journey — because reading is always better when shared.

Featured Book of the Week

“Purple Hibiscus” by Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie

This novel explores the harsh reality of family, faith, and freedom in Nigeria. I loved how the author captures the tension between tradition and independence. If you’re looking for a thought‑provoking read, this is a great place to start.
